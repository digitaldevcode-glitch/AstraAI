Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Introduction

AstraAI (“we”, “our”, “us”) is an astrology app that provides daily horoscopes, natal charts, partner compatibility, an AI astrologer chat, and a dream interpreter. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using the app you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Birth information — date of birth, time of birth, and place (city) of birth that you enter to generate personalized content.
  • Dream descriptions — the text you type to get a dream interpretation.
  • Chat messages — the questions and messages you send to the AI astrologer.

Automatically collected

  • Device permissions — notification permission, microphone (only if you use voice input), and exact alarm access to deliver your scheduled notifications.
  • Local usage data — such as your daily message usage counter, stored only on your device.
  • Advertising identifiers — a device advertising ID may be used by our advertising partners to display and measure ads.

3. How we use your information

  • To generate the personalized horoscopes, analyses, and interpretations you request.
  • To provide and improve the features of the app.
  • To send scheduled daily notifications (if you enable them).
  • To display advertising that keeps the app free to use.

4. Third-party services

We use the following third-party services which may process data on our behalf:

  • AI provider (DeepSeek API). To generate horoscopes, chat replies, natal analyses, and dream interpretations, the relevant text you provide (birth details, dream text, or chat message) is sent to DeepSeek's API. The AI provider processes this data solely to generate the response and does not store it for its own purposes.
  • Advertising networks. We display ads via an advertising SDK. Ad networks may use device identifiers and technical information to serve and measure advertisements. We do not share your birth data or personal content with ad networks.

5. Data storage and retention

  • On-device storage. Your birth data, chat history, dream history, and usage counters are stored locally on your device (Android SharedPreferences).
  • Transient AI processing. Data sent to our AI provider is used only to generate the response and is not retained by us on a server.
  • You can delete this data at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

6. Permissions we request

  • Notifications — to send your daily horoscope and optional interesting reminders.
  • Microphone — only for the optional voice input feature (on supported devices). We do not record or store audio.
  • Exact alarms — to deliver notifications at the time you set.
  • Internet — to communicate with the AI provider and ad networks.

7. Children's privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Data sent to our AI provider is transmitted over HTTPS. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

9. Your rights

  • You can access or correct your information within the app.
  • You can delete your data by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.
  • You can disable notifications and mic permission at any time in the app or your device settings.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising via your device's advertising settings where available.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
